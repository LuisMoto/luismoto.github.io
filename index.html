A different lens

I've always been interested in how information is structured—whether through the rules of a language or the logic of an equation. My dual background in Linguistics (UAM) and Applied Mathematics (UNAM) allows me to approach problems from very different angles.

Outside of numbers and code, I spend a lot of my time on music; I play bass and piano, and I really enjoy sharing those moments with friends. I read constantly, with a special fondness for Latin American authors like Borges and Cortázar, who play with the structure of their own worlds. I'm also an avid chess player and have a soft spot for Asian food, which is always my go-to choice when eating out.
